## The problem
With increased interest in providing AI experiences, we needed a way to provide designers with a ready-made pattern from which to build chatbot interfaces. Up until this point, individual product teams were building interfaces and experiences independent of each other resulting in a variety of solutions and approaches that lacked continuity across experience and branding.
